# KenyaRoadSafetyAnalysis

1.PROBLEM STATEMENT
-Road accidents continue to pose significant safety and economic challenges. The goal of this project is to identify accident
patterns, contributing factors and high-risk conditions using data analytics.

2.DATA CLEANING
-Removing duplicates.
-Creating a surrogate key.
-Handling missing values.
-Standardizing categorical variables.
-Engineered analytical features.

3.KEY FINDINGS
-Accidents peak on Fridays, Thursdays and Saturdays.
-Weekends experience a higher average accidents per day.
-1500hrs to 1700hrs are the busiest hours (peak hours - commuter rush hours).
-Fog and mist conditions produce the highest fatality rates.
-Motorcycles have the highest fatality rates among vehicle categories.
-Rural areas experience fewer accidents compared to urban but records more severe outcomes.
-Fatality rates generally increase as speed limits increase.

4.RECOMMENDATIONS
-Increase enforcement during commuter rush hours.
-Improve rural roads lighting and emergency response infrastructure.
-Strengthen motorcycle safety enforcement and helmet compliance.
-Prioritize monitoring and enforcement on high-speed roads.
